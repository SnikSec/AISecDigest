### [RedDarwin: Evolving Jailbreak Skills against Frontier LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23726)
*arXiv cs.CR · 13h ago · score 92*

RedDarwin is an agentic framework that evolves jailbreak attacks against frontier LLMs by treating attack mechanisms as reusable, composable skills rather than fixed prompts, achieving dramatic improvements in attack success rates (18.8% to 47.3% on AdvBench) and near-perfect performance against current production models. This research demonstrates that systematic evolution and recombination of attack primitives can circumvent existing safeguards and poses a significant red-teaming concern for LLM security practitioners deploying frontier models.
