## AI Identity & Non-Human Identity (1)

### [FastMCP MCP client wrappers construct M2M OAuth providers without issuer binding — client secret redirectable to attacker-advertised authorization server](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-vqqp-x5qm-5qp4)
*GitHub Security Advisories · 17h ago · score 92*

FastMCP versions 4.0.0-4.1.1 fail to bind OAuth client credentials to a specific authorization server issuer, allowing untrusted/compromised MCP servers to redirect credentials to attacker-controlled endpoints. This credential-binding vulnerability is patched in 4.1.2 by adding issuer validation and requiring MCP SDK 2.2.0, preventing token harvesting and misuse in agent/workload identity scenarios.
