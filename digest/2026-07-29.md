### [When Do Agent Loops Mistake Stagnation for Progress? Self-Evaluation Bias and Externally Grounded Verification in Long-Running Autonomous LLM Agent Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25152)
*arXiv cs.AI · 9h ago · score 82*

This research demonstrates that autonomous LLM agents exhibit "progress mirage," mistaking self-perceived improvements for actual progress while real-world outcomes stagnate or regress—a critical failure mode when agents autonomously evaluate their own work without external grounding. The findings show that even sophisticated in-band evaluators accepting full artifact context accept 44% of actual regressions and reject 38% of genuine improvements, highlighting a fundamental vulnerability in long-running agentic systems that could lead to eroded performance in production deployments.
