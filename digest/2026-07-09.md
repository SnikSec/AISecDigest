### [When Agents Remember Too Much: Memory Poisoning Attacks on Large Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06595)
*arXiv cs.AI · 10h ago · score 92*

This paper introduces GhostWriter, a novel memory poisoning attack against LLM-based personal agents that achieve 98% injection rates and 60% activation rates by exploiting unsecured long-term memory subsystems. The research is critical for security practitioners as autonomous agents handling sensitive tasks (email, calendar, code repos) are increasingly deployed, and current memory governance lacks security controls to prevent such attacks.

### [Certifying Ghosts: How Cybersecurity AI Agents Break the EU Cyber Resilience Act](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07109)
*arXiv cs.CR · 10h ago · score 92*

This research analyzes how cybersecurity AI agents undermine the EU Cyber Resilience Act's compliance framework by simultaneously finding vulnerabilities at superhuman scale and speed, rendering traditional risk assessment processes and security certifications obsolete within days of product release. The paper demonstrates that when both defenders and attackers deploy equivalent AI capabilities, compliance mechanisms designed around slow vulnerability discovery and sustainable fix rates become ineffective, necessitating fundamental regulatory redesign rather than process improvements.
