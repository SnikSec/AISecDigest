### [PISmith: Reinforcement Learning-based Red Teaming for Prompt Injection Defenses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13026)
*arXiv cs.CR · 9h ago · score 85*

The work introduces novel techniques (adaptive entropy regularization and dynamic advantage weighting) to overcome reward sparsity challenges in finding effective attacks, providing security engineers with critical evidence that existing prompt injection mitigations require stronger defense mechanisms.
