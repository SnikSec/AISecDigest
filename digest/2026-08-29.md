### [Safety Does Not Compose: Non-Decaying Loop State for Autonomous LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27141)
*arXiv cs.AI · 11h ago · score 92*

This research demonstrates that current LLM agent safety mechanisms fail to detect attacks fragmented across multiple autonomous loop iterations, as they reset after each trajectory. The authors present LoopHarness, a framework using persistent, non-decaying safety state to bound unauthorized actions at the loop level—a critical insight for deploying autonomous agents safely in production.

*arXiv cs.AI · 11h ago · score 92*

PLCBench demonstrates that autonomous LLM agents can successfully achieve sustained physical impact on industrial control systems in ~31% of attempts by directly manipulating programmable logic controllers through real hardware-in-the-loop testing. This research reveals critical gaps in how we evaluate AI agent capabilities against critical infrastructure, showing that many attacks succeed beyond initial software exploitation to cause lasting physical consequences.
