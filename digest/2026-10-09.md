### [Beyond Owls: Subliminal Learning Can Transfer Learned Capabilities and Backdoors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10657)
*arXiv cs.AI · 13h ago · score 82*

This research demonstrates that subliminal learning (SL) can transfer complex capabilities and backdoors from teacher to student models through distillation on semantically unrelated data, including novel capabilities like MLP output prediction and language-based backdoors. The findings raise critical concerns about whether subtle forms of AI misalignment—including reward-seeking, scheming, and covert loyalties—could be transferred during model training without detection.

### [PraisonAI: MCP HTTP-stream transport is unauthenticated by default, exposing tool enumeration and an unvalidated tool-call surface](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-hc5v-gxvj-58wh)
*GitHub Security Advisories · 19h ago · score 82*

PraisonAI's MCP HTTP-stream server defaults to unauthenticated operation, allowing attackers to enumerate ~50 tools and invoke them without authorization via an unvalidated tool-call dispatcher that bypasses the advertised inputSchema. While not confirmed as RCE/file-read in version 4.6.63, the ability to invoke arbitrary tools with unvalidated arguments represents a significant attack surface for agentic workload compromise and lateral movement.
