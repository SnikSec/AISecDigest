### [PlanFlip: Attacking Multi-Agent LLM Systems via Planning-Phase Prompt Injection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16199)
*arXiv cs.AI · 9h ago · score 92*

PlanFlip demonstrates a critical attack surface in multi-agent LLM systems by injecting malicious prompts into the planning phase, achieving cascade amplification across all downstream agents with success rates up to 68% on GPT-5. The research reveals that stronger models are more vulnerable, homogeneous agent architectures create blind spots where Critics fail to detect semantic deviations, and proposes detection defenses that achieve up to 100% detection rates.
