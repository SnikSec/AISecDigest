### [A2ABreak: Systematic Security Analysis of the A2A Protocol](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10871)
*arXiv cs.CR · 11h ago · score 92*

A2ABreak presents the first systematic security analysis of the A2A protocol, a Linux Foundation standard for inter-agent authentication and delegation, uncovering 11 critical vulnerabilities including credential harvesting in delegation chains and context injection attacks. This research is critical for security practitioners as A2A is becoming the foundational communication layer for multi-agent AI systems, and these protocol-level flaws could affect any implementation of the standard.
