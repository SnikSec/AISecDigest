*arXiv cs.CR · 13h ago · score 85*

This paper introduces Provenance-Aware Transformers, an architectural defense against indirect prompt injection attacks by explicitly tracking and enforcing trust boundaries based on input source/origin through learnable ring IDs and origin attention mechanisms. The approach represents a significant departure from standard transformer training by making source authority actionable at the architectural level rather than relying on the model to infer trustworthiness from text patterns alone.
