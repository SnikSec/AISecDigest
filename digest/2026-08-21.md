### [Inadvertent Context Leakage in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19857)
*arXiv cs.CR · 8h ago · score 92*

This research demonstrates that sensitive context data (credentials, health records, financial information) held in an LLM's context window can be inadvertently leaked through the model's benign outputs via hidden correlations, with 2-digit secrets reconstructed with near-perfect accuracy across eight proprietary models. The findings reveal that stronger models leak more due to improved instruction-following, suggesting context leakage is an inherent capability artifact rather than a patchable bug, and enable practical attacks including inference of semantic predicates and extraction of sensitive identifiers from routine model outputs.
