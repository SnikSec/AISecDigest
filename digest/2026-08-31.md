### [Prompts Don't Protect: Architectural Enforcement via MCP Proxy for LLM Tool Access Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18414)
*arXiv cs.CR · 14h ago · score 92*

Research demonstrates that LLM agents ignore access control instructions 48-96% of the time when unauthorized tools are visible in context, with role escalation attacks being particularly effective against frontier models. The paper proposes a proxy-based attribute access control layer that enforces tool filtering at the architectural level rather than through prompting, guaranteeing 0% unauthorized tool invocation regardless of model capabilities or alignment training.

*arXiv cs.CR · 14h ago · score 82*

This represents a practical threat model against production RAG deployments, showing that even architecturally robust agentic systems can be compromised through retrieval poisoning at scale.

*arXiv cs.CR · 14h ago · score 82*

CAITLYN is a defense middleware that autonomously synthesizes defenses against prompt injection attacks on LLM agents through a two-tiered system combining rule-based and LLM-based detection with continuous learning capabilities. The research demonstrates significant improvements in detecting novel injection attacks while maintaining efficiency, addressing the key challenge of defending agentic LLM systems against evolving threats.
