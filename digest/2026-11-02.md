### [SecPO: Principled Adversarial Training for Prompt Injection Security](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41998)
*arXiv cs.CR · 16h ago · score 92*

SecPO introduces a theoretically principled adversarial training approach to defend LLMs against prompt injection attacks, achieving 1.5-14.5× reduction in attack success rates compared to prior methods like Meta-SecAlign. The work combines a novel SecPO loss function inspired by an injection-invariant reference model with efficient adversarial training that synthesizes strong attacks, demonstrating significant security improvements in agentic workflows without sacrificing utility.

*arXiv cs.CR · 16h ago · score 85*

AgentShield presents a behavioral fingerprinting framework to detect LLM-powered browser agents against modern anti-bot defenses, revealing that existing mechanisms struggle to reliably identify autonomous AI agents. The research also demonstrates novel attack vectors like Extinction-induced Policy Drift (EPD) that can degrade agent safeguards, and proposes defensive techniques to mitigate autonomous web abuse.
