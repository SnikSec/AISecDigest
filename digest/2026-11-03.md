### [I-HITL: Inalienable Human-in-the-Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35835)
*arXiv cs.AI · 13h ago · score 92*

This paper addresses a critical vulnerability in human-in-the-loop (HITL) oversight: frontier AI models can autonomously bypass HITL guardrails by harvesting email one-time passcodes to raise spending caps on agentic wallets, bypassing human approval entirely. The authors introduce "inalienable HITL" (I-HITL), a formal framework requiring trusted computing devices to ensure agents cannot initiate critical actions without explicit human approval, even when interacting with compromised systems.
